Info

Data storageEverything (stock, costs, sales, deposits, settings) is stored only in your own browser on this device. Your bazaar, sales, costs and key are never sent to any server run by this app.
Data sharingYour bazaar, sales and deposit data is not shared with anyone. The only data a third party receives is what Google gets when you visit the site, explained under Third parties.
Purpose of useTrack your bazaar stock, sales, profit and deposits, and alert you when something sells.
Key storage and sharingYour API key is saved in this browser only and is sent only to Torn (api.torn.com). This app never sends it anywhere else. Pages can technically read what they store, and this page also loads Google ad code, so use Forget key in the menu if you stop using the app or share the device.
Key access levelLimited Access is needed to import your bazaar, read your cash on hand and track sales. A Public key is enough for market values and lowest prices. Full Access is never needed.
Third partiesGoogle Fonts (the typeface) and Google AdSense (ads) load from Google servers. Google may see your IP address and device details and may set cookies, under its own privacy policy. This app does not send your bazaar data or API key to Google.
API useThe sale tracker checks once every 5 seconds (12 calls a minute). Shop watch, when on, reads Torn's NPC shop stock (torn cityshops) every 10 to 60 seconds. Together this stays well under Torn's limit of 100 per minute. Shop watch only alerts you and never buys anything.

This is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Torn. Sales are estimated from changes in your bazaar stock, so check amounts before you deposit. Deposits are never made automatically.
